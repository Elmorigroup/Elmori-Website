BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful answers.

Can I start from a ready-made layout?

Yes. The planned ordering journey lets you select an available layout, provide your details and review a personalised preview before approval. The archive is still being prepared.

Can I request a custom design?

You can describe a custom project, including the product, style and files you already have. Scope, pricing and delivery will be confirmed directly.

Can I edit a template on this website?

Direct editing is reserved for a future phase. The initial service is selection, personalisation by Elmori and preview approval.

What files will I need?

Have your original logo, final text and suitable images ready. Product-specific artwork requirements will be confirmed before production.
